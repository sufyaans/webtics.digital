## The challenge

Massive Wave Research recruits respondents and runs fieldwork for consumer, healthcare and business research. Its website has two very different visitors: clients looking for a reliable fieldwork partner, and members of the public who want to take part in research.

On top of that, the site is not just a brochure. When a survey goes live, it can be hit by thousands of respondents in a short window as recruitment campaigns go out. A normal small business website, built to handle a trickle of visitors a day, would fall over under that kind of load.

## Our approach

We gave each audience its own clear path. Clients see services, sectors and proof. Respondents see one obvious button: join the panel. Both get their own set of FAQs, so nobody reads answers meant for someone else.

Behind that, the site was custom-built rather than assembled from a standard template, specifically so it could run live marketing surveys and cope with over 5,000 visitors arriving at once without slowing down or falling over. We also took on ongoing SEO management, so the site keeps earning organic traffic between campaigns, not just during them.

## The solution

- A custom-built platform, not a theme, able to handle over 5,000 concurrent respondents on a live survey.
- Service pages for consumer, healthcare and B2B fieldwork.
- A "Join our panel" route for respondents, kept separate from the client journey.
- Ongoing SEO management, so the site ranks between recruitment campaigns as well as during them.
- Separate FAQs for clients and for respondents.
