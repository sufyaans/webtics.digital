## The challenge

Massive Wave Research recruits respondents and runs fieldwork for consumer, healthcare and business research. Its website has two very different visitors: clients looking for a reliable fieldwork partner, and members of the public who want to take part in research.

[CHALLENGE DETAIL TO CONFIRM]

## Our approach

We gave each audience its own clear path. Clients see services, sectors and proof. Respondents see one obvious button: join the panel. Both get their own set of FAQs, so nobody reads answers meant for someone else.

<FullBleed src="/work/massivewave-page.jpg" tall alt="Massive Wave Research website, full home page" caption="The Massive Wave Research home page, hover to scroll through it." />

## The solution

- Service pages for consumer, healthcare and B2B fieldwork.
- A "Join our panel" route for respondents, kept separate from the client journey.
- Case studies and a blog to build trust and bring in search traffic.
- Separate FAQs for clients and for respondents.
