## The challenge

Shutter Inc was a brand-new business. There was no old website to replace, no existing customers to reassure and no brand recognition to lean on. The site had to do two jobs at once: look as established and trustworthy as a shutter company that had been fitting homes for years, and sell something people normally want to see and touch before they buy.

Farhan came to the project with, in his words, zero knowledge about websites. An off-the-shelf theme would have been the easy route, but it would have made Shutter Inc look like every other local trades site.

## Our approach

We kept the focus on the customer experience. A homeowner visiting the site wants to know three things: what the shutters look like, how the service works and how to get started. The site answers them in that order.

Rather than drop in a stock WordPress theme, we built custom Elementor layouts and hand-written code throughout, so the finished site reads as bespoke rather than assembled from a template. We worked through it together on weekly Zoom calls and WhatsApp, so every decision was explained and nothing came as a surprise.

## The solution

- Built on WordPress with Elementor, using custom layouts and code rather than an off-the-shelf theme.
- A home page that leads with the product and a clear "Explore our range" route.
- A four-step "How we work" section: get in touch, a home visit, made to measure, expertly fitted.
- Pages for the range and the styles, so visitors can compare options.
- Two ways to enquire on every page: request a brochure or book a design consultation.
