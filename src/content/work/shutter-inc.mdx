## The challenge

Shutter Inc sells something people want to see before they buy: UK-made shutters, measured and fitted in the customer's home. The website had to do the job of a showroom, showing the styles and finishes clearly and giving visitors a simple next step.

Farhan came to the project with, in his words, zero knowledge about websites. So the process needed to be as clear as the result.

## Our approach

We kept the focus on the customer experience. A homeowner visiting the site wants to know three things: what the shutters look like, how the service works and how to get started. The site answers them in that order.

We worked through it together on weekly Zoom calls and WhatsApp, so every decision was explained and nothing came as a surprise.

<FullBleed src="/work/shutterinc-page.jpg" tall alt="Shutter Inc website, full home page" caption="The Shutter Inc home page, hover to scroll through it." />

## The solution

- A home page that leads with the product and a clear "Explore our range" route.
- A four-step "How we work" section: get in touch, a home visit, made to measure, expertly fitted.
- Pages for the range and the styles, so visitors can compare options.
- Two ways to enquire on every page: request a brochure or book a design consultation.
