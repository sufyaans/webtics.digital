## The challenge

The Meetings Space runs forums where event buyers and suppliers meet face to face, in destinations across Europe. Its old website was dated and did not reflect the energy of the events themselves. It also had to do real practical work: list upcoming forums, explain what buyers and suppliers each get, and let people register and log in, not just look good.

## Our approach

This was a full rebuild from scratch, not a redesign of the old pages. We designed the new site in Figma first, then built it on Next.js and Node.js rather than a page-builder platform, so the interactions and the bold, vibrant visual style could be built exactly as designed rather than bent to fit a template.

We let the events do the talking. Full-screen photography and oversized type set the tone, and the next forums are never more than a scroll away. Buyers and suppliers each have their own page, because they come with different questions.

## The solution

- Designed in Figma, then built on Next.js and Node.js for full control over the bright, vibrant style.
- A striking home page built around event photography.
- An events calendar with a page for each forum.
- Separate journeys for buyers and suppliers, plus a rates page.
- Registration and member log-in.
