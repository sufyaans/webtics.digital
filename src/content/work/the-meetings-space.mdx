## The challenge

The Meetings Space runs forums where event buyers and suppliers meet face to face, in destinations across Europe. The website has to sell the energy of those events, and also do practical work: list upcoming forums, explain what buyers and suppliers each get, and let people register and log in.

[CHALLENGE DETAIL TO CONFIRM]

## Our approach

We let the events do the talking. Full-screen photography and oversized type set the tone, and the next forums are never more than a scroll away. Buyers and suppliers each have their own page, because they come with different questions.

## The solution

- A striking home page built around event photography.
- An events calendar with a page for each forum.
- Separate journeys for buyers and suppliers, plus a rates page.
