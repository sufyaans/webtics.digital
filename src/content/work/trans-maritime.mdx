## The challenge

Trans Maritime moves cargo by sea, air, road and rail, and also handles warehousing, customs clearance and project cargo. That is a lot to explain. The website needed to present every service clearly, look as dependable as the business itself and give customers a quick route to enquire.

[CHALLENGE DETAIL TO CONFIRM: what they had before and what was not working.]

## Our approach

We organised the site around what a customer is trying to ship and how. Each solution has its own page, reached from one simple menu, so nobody has to dig for the service they need.

<FullBleed src="/work/transmaritime-page.jpg" tall alt="Trans Maritime website, full home page" caption="The Trans Maritime home page, hover to scroll through it." />

## The solution

- A bold home page that says what the company does in one line.
- A dedicated page for each solution: sea, air and road freight, warehousing and distribution, customs clearance, the inland container depot, consolidation and project cargo.
- An FAQ and a short contact form, with click-to-call and email links throughout.
- A layout that works as well on a phone as on a desktop.
