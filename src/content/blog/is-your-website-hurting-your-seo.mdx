You can have great products and good reviews and still be invisible on Google because of the website itself. Here are seven signs to look for, and how to check each one.

## 1. It is slow on a phone

Open your site on your phone over mobile data. If it takes more than a few seconds to show anything useful, visitors leave and rankings suffer. Run it through Google's PageSpeed Insights for a score.

## 2. Pages are not indexed

In Google Search Console, the Pages report shows which pages Google has and has not indexed, and why.

## 3. Every page has the same title

Page titles are one of the strongest signals you control. Search `site:yourdomain.co.uk` on Google and look at the titles that come up.

## 4. Several pages compete for the same search

If three pages all target "web design Leicester", Google has to guess which one matters. Give each page one clear job.

## 5. A redesign broke your old links

If you have redesigned in the last few years and traffic dropped, old URLs may not redirect to the new ones. Search Console will show them as "Not found (404)".

## 6. There is nothing to answer real questions

Service pages that only list services rarely rank. Pages that answer what customers actually ask, such as costs, timelines and comparisons, often do.

## 7. Nothing is measured

If you cannot see which searches bring visitors and which pages bring enquiries, you cannot improve either.
