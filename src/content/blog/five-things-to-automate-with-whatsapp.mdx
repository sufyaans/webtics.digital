Many of your customers already prefer to message rather than call. With the WhatsApp Business Platform, some of those conversations can run themselves, while you stay in control of the ones that matter.

## 1. Answer common questions instantly

Opening hours, prices, parking, what to bring. A simple assistant can answer these at any hour and hand anything unusual to you.

## 2. Take bookings without phone tag

Customers pick a time, get a confirmation and the booking lands straight in your calendar.

## 3. Send reminders that cut no-shows

An automatic reminder the day before, with an easy way to confirm or rearrange, is one of the simplest wins in this list.

<PullQuote>
  The best automation is the one your customers do not notice. It just feels like quick service.
</PullQuote>

## 4. Capture and qualify leads

Ask the same three or four questions you would ask on the phone, then pass a tidy summary to you or straight into your CRM.

## 5. Keep customers updated

Order dispatched, job booked, engineer on the way. Short, useful updates reduce "just checking" calls.

<Callout title="Keep a human in the loop">
  Every automation we build has a clear "talk to a person" route, and alerts you when a conversation needs attention.
</Callout>

## What to check before you start

- WhatsApp charges per conversation, and the rules on business messaging are strict. Make sure customers have opted in.
- Decide who picks up handed-over conversations, and how fast.
- Log everything, so nothing is lost if a message fails.
